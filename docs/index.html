Never have I ever, for your group chat.

Pick a pack, drop one link in the chat. Everyone taps I have, Never or Pass on their own phone. Answers stay sealed until everyone has played, then the reveal shows who has, who never, and who's the only one.

Play free in your browser

Support

Something not working, or a statement you'd like removed? Open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/never-ever/issues. Please don't include anyone's answers.

Lost access to a round after switching browsers? Ask the person who started it to remove your old nickname, then join again.
