    How it works

    1. Start a round

      Pick a pack, say how many are playing and when the reveal is. You answer first.

    2. Share one link

      Paste it in WhatsApp, iMessage or any group chat. Friends type a nickname and tap through 10 statements.

    3. The reveal

      Once everyone has answered (or at your deadline), it plays statement by statement, then the end card: most experienced, most innocent, answer twins.

    Questions

    Why not just play it in the chat?

    In a chat everyone sees the first replies and copies the herd, or holds back. In Fess Up nobody sees anyone's answers until the reveal, so people answer honestly, and the app spots the surprises for you.

    Who sees my answers?

    Nobody, until the reveal. After that, everyone in the round who has also answered. Choose Anonymous reveal when you start a round to show only counts ("2 of 7 have…"), never names.

    Do I have to answer everything?

    No. Pass is always there and shows only as a pass.

    Is it OK for family and work chats?

    Yes. Every pack is PG-13 and written by us, with no sex or drug questions. The Family and Work packs are written to be safe with parents, grandparents and colleagues.

    When does the reveal happen?

    When the number of players you set have answered, or at your deadline, whichever comes first. The person who started the round can also reveal early once two people have answered.

    Do I need an account or an app?

    No. It's a web page: the link opens in any browser, including the one inside WhatsApp or Instagram. Your nickname is remembered on your device.

    How long is a round kept?

    Rounds are deleted automatically 90 days after they start, and the person who started a round can delete it at any time.
